Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2024

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

We collect information you provide directly when using Explore Comet, including search queries, interaction patterns, and preferences. This data helps us deliver personalized, anticipatory experiences through our cognitive interaction systems.

Technical information such as browser type, device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage statistics are automatically collected to improve our predictive intelligence and ensure optimal performance.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Data is processed securely and used solely to enhance your experience with our empathetic algorithms.

Cookies and Tracking

Explore Comet uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, analyze usage patterns, and enable our neural dialogue features. Essential cookies are required for core functionality, while optional cookies enhance personalization.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may limit some features of our conversational search and intuitive discovery capabilities.

Third-party cookies from trusted partners help us understand how you interact with our platform and improve our anticipatory AI systems.

Analytics and Performance

We use analytics tools to measure performance, understand user behavior, and refine our predictive intelligence. This includes tracking page views, session duration, feature usage, and interaction patterns with our cognitive systems.

Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized whenever possible. Individual user activities are analyzed only to improve personalized recommendations and anticipatory responses.

Performance monitoring helps us maintain fast, reliable service and optimize our neural dialogue capabilities for better user experiences.

Your Rights Under GDPR

As a user, you have comprehensive rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access:Request copies of your personal data
  • Right to Rectification:Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to Erasure:Request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to Restrict Processing:Limit how we use your data
  • Right to Data Portability:Receive your data in a structured format
  • Right to Object:Oppose processing of your personal data
  • Right to Withdraw Consent:Revoke permission at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information provided below.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes encryption, secure servers, and regular security audits.

While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We continuously update our security practices to address emerging threats.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. When data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymized.

Some information may be retained longer if required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as dispute resolution and security.

Third-Party Services

Explore Comet may integrate with third-party services to enhance functionality. These partners have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. We carefully select partners who maintain high privacy standards.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services linked from our platform.
